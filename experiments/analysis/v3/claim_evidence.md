## Terminology used below (all interval constructions are named explicitly)
- **AR-SCP**: split conformal with the absolute-residual score |y − ŷ| on a point model's prediction.
- **EN-SCP / DN-SCP / GN-SCP**: split conformal with a spread-normalised score |y − μ| / max(s, floor), where s is the 5-member ensemble SD (EN), the 200-pass MC-dropout SD (DN), or the Gaussian σ (GN); μ is the ensemble mean, dropout mean or Gaussian mean respectively.
- **Raw bands**: μ ± z·s with no calibration (Gaussian-reference nominal level).

All SCP variants use the same 9 calibration recordings (31,878 overlapping windows), the `horizon_channel` threshold (one per step × channel), and the order statistic ⌈(n+1)(1−α)⌉. Because the windows overlap and come from 9 recordings, the exchangeability assumption behind the finite-sample marginal guarantee is **not established**: nominal level, empirical coverage and theoretical guarantee are three different things. In-distribution empirical coverage is reported as a measurement, not as a verification or refutation of a guarantee.
